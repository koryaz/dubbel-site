Sober You vs Drunk You

Lock apps before a night out.

Before you go out, pick the apps you don't want to open later and leave a short note to your later self. When you try to open one of them that night, Dubbel's screen appears with your note and a short delay. In the morning you get a recap of the night, and anything you typed but didn't send waits for a sober decision.

Dubbel runs entirely on your iPhone. No account, no server, no analytics. Your notes, your app choices and your nights never leave the device.

Dubbel is in limited testing on TestFlight. The App Store release for the United States and the United Kingdom is coming soon.
